What makes it feel premium

Formatting without MarkdownA floating bar plus ⌘B/⌘I. Typing "- " or "[] " turns into a list or checklist, and no symbols ever show.
Quick note from anywhere⌥Space opens a small floating note over whatever app she's in, like Spotlight but for notes.
Small touchesSpring animations, satisfying checkbox ticks, haptics on the trackpad, highlighter colors, and a calm dark mode.
